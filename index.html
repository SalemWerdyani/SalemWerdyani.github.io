Research interest

A multidisciplinary approach

How do genetic variations shape disease susceptibility, progression, and therapeutic response, and how can these genomic signals be leveraged to guide precision medicine?

01 · Genetics

Human Genetics

GWAS, WES/WGS, and single-cell RNA-seq data analysis to identify genetic architecture underlying complex diseases, from osteoarthritis to hearing loss to hereditary cancer.

02 · Computation

Bioinformatics & AI

Machine and deep learning models, from Random Forest to TensorFlow and JAX, built to prioritize and functionally annotate variants at scale.

03 · Inference

Statistical Genomics

Polygenic risk scoring (PRS) analyses, fine-mapping (SuSiE, FINEMAP), and causal inference eQTL (cTWAS) to move from association to candidate causal gene.

04 · Integration

Multi-Omics analyses

Integrating genomic, transcriptomic, single-cell RNA-seq data, proteomic, and metabolomic data with clinical outcomes for patient stratification and precision medicine.

Postdoctoral Fellow

Khan Lab, Faculty of Medicine · The University of Chicago
  • Led research on germline genetic influences on CAR T-cell therapy outcomes using a clinico-genomic DLBCL cohort from the UChicago Biobank.
